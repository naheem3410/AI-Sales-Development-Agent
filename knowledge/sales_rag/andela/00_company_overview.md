# Andela — Company overview

## Snapshot
Andela is known for connecting organizations with **remote engineering and technology talent**, historically with strong roots in **Africa**, and more recently emphasizing **global talent networks** and **AI-related capabilities** (verify current headlines on andela.com). Buyers are typically engineering leaders, CTOs, and heads of talent who need **speed and quality** when scaling product teams.

## Positioning (high level)
- **Theme:** Access vetted technical talent and delivery capacity without limiting hiring to a single geography.
- **Buyer motion:** Often starts with engineering pain (delivery timelines, hiring velocity), expands with successful squads or long-term placements.

## Why prospects care
- Hiring senior engineers in competitive markets is slow and expensive.
- Distributed delivery models require **trust** — vetting, communication, and clear ways of working.

## Demo / outreach angle (for our sales narrative)
When messaging Andela-aligned buyers, anchor on **velocity to shipped work**, **quality bar for engineers**, **flexible engagement models**, and **scaling product roadmaps** without proportional headcount growth.
