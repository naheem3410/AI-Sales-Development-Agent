# Andela — ICP & personas (for outbound targeting)

## Strong fits (titles / functions)
- **Engineering:** CTO, VP Engineering, Head of Engineering, Director of Engineering, Platform leads.
- **Talent / HR tech buyers:** VP Talent, Head of Technical Recruiting (when evaluating talent partners).
- **Product leadership:** CPO / VP Product when roadmap pressure forces engineering capacity conversations.

## Company signals
- Rapid **product roadmap** expansion; frequent **engineering hiring**.
- Organizations adopting **distributed teams** or hybrid models.

## Geography
- Global enterprises and growth-stage companies hiring across regions.

## Disqualifiers / caution
- Companies with **frozen hiring** or outsourcing bans.
- Highly regulated environments where **offshore / vendor access** faces legal barriers without prior approval.

## Pain hooks
- Missed roadmap dates due to staffing gaps.
- Interview funnel fatigue; low signal-to-noise from job boards.
- Need for **specific stacks** or seniority bands that are scarce locally.
