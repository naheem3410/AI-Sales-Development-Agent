# Andela — Messaging & positioning guidelines

## Voice
- Respectful, **competence-forward** — engineers and engineering leaders tune out hype.
- Emphasize **delivery**, **risk reduction**, and **clarity of engagement**.

## Do say
- “Teams scaling product delivery often evaluate partners like Andela when **time-to-hire** breaks the roadmap.”
- “Useful when you need **senior contributors** without expanding local recruiting capacity.”

## Don’t say
- Guarantees about individual engineer performance unless contractually true.
- Anything that sounds like **exploitative** outsourcing framing — modern buyers reject it.

## Competitive framing (neutral)
Andela sits alongside **traditional agencies**, **boutique consultancies**, **nearshore shops**, and **global talent marketplaces**. Differentiate our product by what **we** uniquely automate or improve in outreach — not by attacking competitors.
