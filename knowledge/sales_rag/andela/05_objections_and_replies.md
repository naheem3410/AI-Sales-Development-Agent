# Andela — Objections & suggested replies

## “We only hire full-time employees.”
**Reply angle:** Acknowledge values fit; pivot to cases where **contract capacity** unblocks roadmap while FTE reqs run in parallel — only if honest for your buyer.

## “Distributed teams didn’t work for us before.”
**Reply angle:** Probe **what failed** (communication, timezone overlap, specs). Position improvements in **ways of working**, tooling, or governance — avoid blaming prior vendors.

## “Security / compliance blocks external engineering.”
**Reply angle:** Offer phased access (non-prod), background checks, VPN constraints — align with customer policy.

## “Cheaper offshore options exist.”
**Reply angle:** Shift from price to **risk-adjusted velocity** — rework cost, missed launches, tech debt — stay factual.

## “We’re reducing headcount, not adding.”
**Reply angle:** Respect freeze; explore **later-stage** pilot or smaller scoped spike **only if** aligned with reality.
