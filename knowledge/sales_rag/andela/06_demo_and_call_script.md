# Andela — Demo & discovery call script (outline)

## Opening
- Confirm product stage, engineering org size, hiring constraints (freeze vs selective growth).
- Clarify stacks and seniority gaps.

## Discovery questions
1. What caused the last roadmap slip — staffing, priorities, or dependencies?
2. How do you evaluate external engineering partners today?
3. What would **success in 90 days** look like for capacity (features shipped, incidents reduced, etc.)?

## Demo flow (adapt to your product)
1. Show **personalization** using approved positioning for **engineering-leader** personas.
2. Show **objection-aware** snippets drawn from this knowledge pack.

## Close
- Align on pilot scope and stakeholders (CTO vs Head of Talent).

## Follow-up
- Summarize pains, metrics, timeline; attach one-pager if applicable.
