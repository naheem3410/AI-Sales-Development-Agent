# HubSpot — Company overview

## Snapshot
HubSpot is a publicly traded software company best known for **CRM** and **inbound marketing**, with integrated hubs for **marketing**, **sales**, **customer service**, **content (CMS)**, and **operations**. It targets organizations that want a unified customer platform rather than stitching together many point tools.

## Positioning (high level)
- **Theme:** Grow better — help businesses attract, engage, and delight customers with connected data across teams.
- **Buyer motion:** Often evaluated by marketing and revenue leaders jointly; IT/procurement becomes involved for integrations, SSO, and security at mid-market and enterprise.

## Why prospects care
- Single source of truth for contacts and companies (CRM object model).
- Workflow automation across marketing campaigns, sales sequences, and support tickets.
- Large ecosystem (marketplace, integrations, academies, certifications).

## Geography & motion
- Global footprint; serves SMB through enterprise with tiered products and services.

## Demo / outreach angle (for our sales narrative)
When speaking to HubSpot-aligned buyers, anchor on **unified revenue data**, **pipeline hygiene**, **campaign attribution**, and **scaling outbound without breaking personalization** — problems HubSpot users feel even after adopting the platform.
