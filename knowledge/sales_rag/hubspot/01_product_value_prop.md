# HubSpot — Product & value proposition (reference)

## Core product pillars (names may evolve — verify on hubspot.com)
1. **Marketing Hub** — campaigns, automation, SEO/content tools, email, analytics.
2. **Sales Hub** — CRM, sequences, forecasting, meetings, deeper sales acceleration features on higher tiers.
3. **Service Hub** — ticketing, knowledge base, automation for customer success.
4. **CMS Hub** — website and landing pages tied to CRM data.
5. **Operations Hub** — data quality, programmable automation, sync across tools.

## Value proposition (how buyers describe outcomes)
- **Faster revenue cycles** when marketing and sales share one database.
- **Better conversion** through nurture and lead scoring (where configured).
- **Operational efficiency** via workflows instead of manual handoffs.

## Differentiators (buyer language)
- **Ease of use** relative to legacy enterprise suites (common perception in SMB/mid-market).
- **Education-led** brand: Academy, certifications, templates — lowers adoption friction for teams.

## Mapping to our offering (generic sales bridge)
Use phrases like: “works alongside your CRM,” “doesn’t replace HubSpot — **augments outbound** where HubSpot sequences stop,” “keeps messaging **on-brand** using your approved positioning.” Avoid claiming official partnership unless contracted.
