# HubSpot — ICP & personas (for outbound targeting)

## Strong fits (titles / functions)
- **Marketing:** VP/Director Marketing, Growth, Demand Gen, Marketing Ops, Campaign Managers.
- **Sales:** VP Sales, RevOps, Sales Managers, SDR/BDR Managers (especially where outbound volume matters).
- **Operations:** Revenue Operations, CRM admins, Marketing Ops supporting tooling and attribution.

## Company signals
- **Teams actively using HubSpot or evaluating consolidation** around CRM + automation.
- **Outbound + inbound mix** — needs consistent messaging across sequences and campaigns.

## Geographic focus
- Global; prioritize regions where your team can support language and calling hours.

## Disqualifiers / caution
- Pure IT infrastructure buyers with no marketing/sales stakeholder.
- Organizations that have **fully standardized** on a competing suite and locked multi-year — unless a specific wedge (new motion, new region, new product line).

## Persona pain hooks
- **Marketing:** attribution fights, low SQL quality, tool sprawl, campaign scaling without more headcount.
- **Sales:** bad lead quality, sequence fatigue, inconsistent talk tracks.
- **RevOps:** duplicate records, broken handoffs, reporting that doesn’t reconcile.
