# HubSpot — Messaging & positioning guidelines

## Voice
- Professional, helpful, **education-forward** — mirrors HubSpot’s brand without impersonating it.
- Lead with **customer outcomes** (pipeline, meetings, conversion), not buzzwords.

## Do say
- “Aligned with how modern revenue teams run HubSpot…”
- “Keeps outbound **consistent** with your positioning and approvals.”
- “Reduces manual research before **personalization**.”

## Don’t say
- Implying **official HubSpot endorsement** without written permission.
- Guaranteed ROI or pipeline multiples without proof.
- “Replaces HubSpot” unless that is literally true for your product.

## Competitive framing (neutral)
HubSpot often competes with Salesforce ecosystems, Adobe/Marketo-class MAPs, and point outbound tools. Position our layer as **workflow + intelligence above the CRM**, not a rip-and-replace.

## Proof points to prepare (fill with your real data)
- Time-to-first-campaign, reply rates vs baseline, reduction in research hours, security posture (SOC2, GDPR), integration list.
