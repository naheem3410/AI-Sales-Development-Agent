# HubSpot — Objections & suggested replies

## “We’re happy with native HubSpot sequences.”
**Reply angle:** Acknowledge strength of native delivery; focus on **upstream quality** — account selection, personalization sources, brand-safe copy at scale — whatever your product objectively improves.

## “No budget this quarter.”
**Reply angle:** Offer a narrow pilot tied to **one concrete metric** (meetings booked, reply rate on a named segment, hours saved).

## “Security review will take months.”
**Reply angle:** Provide **SOC2 / DPIA / data flow** one-pager; propose sandbox with synthetic data.

## “Legal won’t let us use AI for customer emails.”
**Reply angle:** Human-in-the-loop by default; **approved snippets** from RAG; full audit trail if you have it.

## “Our data in HubSpot is messy.”
**Reply angle:** Position RevOps cleanup as parallel workstream; your tool may still add value on **net-new outbound** or **clean cohorts** first.

## “We tried AI outreach — poor results.”
**Reply angle:** Differentiate on **grounded context**, evaluation methodology, and guardrails — cite how you measure quality.
